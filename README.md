# 50-State License Plate Game

A road-trip game: spot a license plate from every US state.

- **`index.html`**: tap-to-color map for a phone. Tap a state when you spot its plate. Pinch or use the + and − buttons to zoom, and your progress is saved in the browser.
- **`printable-map.pdf`**: paper version. Page 1 is the glue board (print on white). Pages 2–6 each hold 10 states to print on red, blue, green, yellow and orange paper, cut out and glue on.

The five colors are arranged so that no two neighboring states share a color.

State outlines come from public-domain US Census boundary data.
